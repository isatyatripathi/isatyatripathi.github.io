# Portfolio strategy

## Audience and outcome

The portfolio is designed for recruiters, hiring managers, engineering leaders,
technical interviewers, and professional peers. Its first-screen job is to make
Satya's seniority, architecture depth, people-leadership scope, and measurable
engineering impact understandable in about 30 seconds.

## Positioning

**Balanced target:** Senior Staff Engineer and Engineering Manager.

**Value proposition:** Satya builds scalable, dependable systems and creates the
clarity, engineering practices, and coaching that help distributed teams deliver.

## Primary proof points

- 10 years in software engineering.
- 30+ production releases with zero rollbacks.
- 89% lower release time-to-market.
- 91% lower production failure rate.
- 73% improvement in Customer Happiness Index.
- 9 engineers line-managed and 10+ mentored.

Internal product and platform names are intentionally generalized. No telephone
number is published.

## Information architecture

1. Hero and recruiter actions
2. Career metrics
3. About
4. Experience
5. Selected impact
6. Core Expertise
7. Engineering leadership
8. Recognition, education, certifications, and publication
9. Resume
10. Contact

Projects, testimonials, volunteer work, and analytics are omitted in the first
release because there is not enough approved source material or they were not
requested.

## Visual system

- Deep navy and charcoal base surfaces
- Magenta-purple accent inspired by the supplied portrait
- High-contrast typography and restrained borders
- Rounded but professional cards
- Light and dark themes with saved preference
- Subtle reveal interactions that honor reduced-motion settings
- Mobile-first responsive layouts and a full-screen mobile menu

## Recruiter conversion path

The header and hero make three actions immediately available: download the
resume, scan the experience, and connect on LinkedIn. Email, LinkedIn, GitHub,
and a dedicated resume section remain available later in the page.
